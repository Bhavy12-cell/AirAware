# 🌍 AirAware – AI Air Quality & Climate Awareness Assistant

AirAware is an AI-powered environmental awareness platform that helps users understand air quality, analyze AQI conditions, and make informed decisions about outdoor activities.

The project combines **Gemini AI, Prompt Engineering, RAG, AQI analysis, trend analysis, and intelligent alerts** to convert complex air-quality information into simple and actionable guidance.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

People often find Air Quality Index (AQI) values difficult to understand and may not know what actions to take when air quality becomes unhealthy.

AirAware aims to bridge this gap by providing understandable AQI information, AI-powered analysis, personalized activity guidance, and environmental awareness.

### How might we use AI to convert air-quality information into simple, actionable awareness so that communities can make more informed decisions about outdoor activities?

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌫️ AQI Dashboard
- Current AQI information
- AQI category
- Pollution indicators
- Selected city information
- Health and safety awareness

### 🤖 AirAware AI Assistant
Users can ask natural-language questions such as:

> "Can I go jogging today?"

The AI analyzes the available AQI information and provides a simple awareness-based response.

### 📚 RAG Knowledge Base
AirAware retrieves relevant information from its air-quality knowledge base before generating responses.

The knowledge base can contain:

- AQI guidelines
- Outdoor activity guidance
- Air pollution awareness
- School and children safety information
- PM2.5 and PM10 information
- Climate and pollution information
- Ways to reduce air pollution

### 📈 AQI Trend Analysis
- 7-day AQI trends
- Average AQI
- Highest and lowest AQI
- Trend analysis
- AI-generated trend summary

### 🏃 Activity Check
Users can check air-quality conditions for activities such as:

- Jogging
- Walking
- Cycling
- Running
- Outdoor sports
- Indoor exercise

### 🚨 AQI Alert System
When AQI reaches an unhealthy level, AirAware can display an alert and generate a warning sound.

Features include:

- AQI warning
- Audio alert
- Stop alert button
- Manual mute control
- Alert severity levels

### 🏫 School Safety Mode
Provides simple air-quality awareness for:

- Students
- Teachers
- Schools
- Outdoor activities

### 🌍 Climate Awareness
Educational information about:

- Air pollution
- Climate change
- Sustainable communities
- Individual environmental actions
